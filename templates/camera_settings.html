ONE-TIME WINDOWS SETUP

Get your phone ready

  1. Enable connected cameraWindows 11 Settings → Bluetooth & devices → Mobile devices → Manage devices → your phone → Use as connected camera.
  2. Allow camera accessOn your Android phone, allow Link to Windows to use the camera. Keep your phone connected and accept its camera prompt.
  3. Choose and previewStart with device 2, your original project setting. If the preview is wrong or unavailable, try another device number. Indices vary by computer.
  4. Start checking inOnce the phone preview works, open the attendance station. Face the phone camera and blink or turn naturally.
